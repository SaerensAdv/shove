Who we are

SHOVE is made by Saerens Advertising, Belgium (company number BE 1019.436.742). Questions: axel@saerensadvertising.com.

The short version

SHOVE has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Your coins, plushies and settings are saved on your own device. We don't receive them.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (coins, level, collection, daily tasks, settings) is kept in your browser's or phone's local storage. In the app, a copy is kept in the phone's app storage so iOS doesn't lose it. Delete the app or clear the site data and it is gone. A backup code only exists if you copy it yourself.

Third parties in the web version

The web version loads fonts from Google Fonts and the game engine from jsDelivr. Like any website, those services see your IP address and browser details when your device downloads the files. The app version bundles everything and makes no such requests, apart from fonts.

Notifications

The app can show one daily reminder at 19:00 if you switch it on. It is scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. Switch it off in Settings.

Ads and purchases (not active yet)

This prototype shows no real ads and takes no payments. If rewarded ads or in-app purchases are added, they will be optional, consent will be asked where the law requires it, and this page will list the providers before release.

Children

SHOVE contains simulated coin-pusher gameplay and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to view, correct or delete. Your data stays under your control on your device. You can contact us or the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) at any time.
